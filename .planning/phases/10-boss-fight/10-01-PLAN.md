# 10-01 — Surface Stack foundation (the no-page-break living place)

## Objective
Introduce the **surface-stack** primitive that turns in-app navigation from route-swaps into **surfaces that rise over a persistent living world**, and prove it by making the **player rise** as a surface (close → recedes to exact prior scroll). This is the floor of the whole "living place" milestone. INCREMENTAL + SAFE — do NOT rip out all routes; add the primitive + convert ONE flow as proof.

## Tasks (2-3, atomic)
1. **Build `SurfaceStack`** (`src/components/system/SurfaceStack.tsx` + a `useSurfaces` context): a state-driven stack of layered surfaces rendered above the persistent shell. API: `push(surface)`, `pop()`, `replace()`. Each surface rises (translateY/opacity, ease `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)`) and recedes on pop. The world underneath never unmounts. Surfaces are kept mounted while stacked (display toggle, not unmount) so scroll + state are preserved.
2. **Scroll/state preservation**: each surface owns a scroll container; pushing a new surface over it preserves the underlying scroll position + React state (keep-alive via mounted-but-hidden, NOT unmount). Returning (pop) restores the exact view — no reset, no white flash.
3. **Prove it with the player**: route the existing full-screen player through `SurfaceStack.push` (rises over the world) instead of the current overlay; close = `pop` → recedes to the exact prior scroll position of the page beneath. Keep the existing VideoPlayer/usePlayer logic; only change HOW it mounts (as a rising surface).
